Our Story

A Philosophy of Refinement

At The One House, every cup is a ceremony. We source single-origin beans from the world's most coveted estates, roasted in small batches to preserve the terroir of each harvest.

Our space was designed for those who understand that luxury is not excess — it is the perfect absence of everything unnecessary. Stay a moment. Stay an afternoon.
